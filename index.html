Listening

Train your ears every day.

Your challenge: Listen for 10–15 minutes every morning. Choose an English-speaking city and listen while you have breakfast, get ready, commute, exercise or prepare for work.

  • Focus on the general topic.
  • Notice words and expressions you recognize.
  • Listen for repeated expressions.
  • Don't translate everything.
Open Radio Garden ↗
🎯 Goal: Understand the message, not every single word.
Speaking

Stop thinking. Start talking.

Use ChatGPT for regular English conversations and Episoden for real 1-to-1 conversations.

  • ChatGPT: 3–4 conversations per week.
  • Episoden: 2–3 conversations per week.
  • Prepare one opinion, one example and one question.
  • Build answers: Answer → Explain → Example → Opinion.
Practice with ChatGPT ↗Open Episoden ↗
💬 Try: “Let's have a 10-minute conversation in English. Ask me questions about different topics. Don't correct me immediately. At the end, give me feedback on my grammar, vocabulary, fluency, and natural ways to express my ideas.”
Natural English

Learn how people really speak.

Search for an expression in PlayPhrase and hear how it is used in movie and TV scenes.

  • Listen to several examples.
  • Notice pronunciation, rhythm and intonation.
  • Notice words around the expression.
  • Imitate it.
  • Create your own sentence.
Open PlayPhrase ↗
🧠 3-step method:
1. LISTEN
2. IMITATE
3. USE
Reading

Read real English.

Follow The Guardian and The New York Times on Instagram and choose content that genuinely interests you.

  • 10–15 minutes a day.
  • First reading: identify the main idea without a dictionary.
  • Second reading: find vocabulary, expressions, collocations and phrasal verbs.
  • Choose 2–3 expressions and create your own sentences.

You don't need hours. Give English 30 focused minutes across your day.

Practice using the expressions you discovered that day.
